# Privacy Policy for DermaMind AI

**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

DermaMind AI ("we", "our", or "the app") provides AI-powered general health, skincare, nutrition, and wellness informational guidance. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the DermaMind AI mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. User Profile & Health Information (Entered by You)

When using the app, you may optionally provide:

* **Basic Profile Details:** Display name, age, height, weight.
* **Calculated Metrics:** Body Mass Index (BMI).
* **Preferences & Attributes:** Skin type (e.g., Oily, Dry, Combination, Normal, Sensitive), fitness goals, and dietary preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Keto, etc.).
* **Allergies & Sensitivities:** Self-reported ingredient or food allergies.

### B. Photos & Images (Skin AI & Food Scanner)

* **Facial and Skin Photos:** When you capture or upload photos for AI Skin Analysis or Photo Enhancement.
* **Meal Photos:** When you capture or upload meal photos for the Food Scanner.
* **Processing:** Photos are transmitted securely via encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to AI models (such as Google Gemini) to generate visual observations and nutrition/skincare suggestions.

### C. Health Assistant Conversations

* Health questions, symptom inquiries, and chat interactions you submit to the AI Health Assistant.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to:

* Generate personalized wellness, nutrition, and skincare recommendations.
* Calculate approximate BMI and fitness guidance.
* Maintain your scan and analysis history locally on your device for your personal reference.
* Synchronize your user profile parameters with Firestore (if cloud sync is enabled).

**We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to advertisers or third parties.**

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

* **Local Storage:** Your analysis logs, meal history, and local preferences are stored securely on your device using a local SQLite/Room database.
* **Cloud Storage:** User profile attributes are synchronized with Google Firebase/Firestore via secure authenticated connections.
* **Data Security:** All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
* **Data Retention:** Information is retained on your device or linked cloud profile until you choose to delete it.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

* **In-App Deletion:** You can delete individual scan history records or clear complete history categories at any time from the **History** screen.
* **Profile Reset:** You can edit or clear your personal attributes in the **Profile** screen.
* **Full Data Deletion Request:** You may request complete deletion of any cloud-associated account data by contacting our support team at `support@dermamind.ai`.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

DermaMind AI utilizes the following trusted third-party service providers:

* **Google AI / Gemini API:** Used for natural language processing, visual skin pattern analysis, and nutritional recognition.
* **Google Firebase:** Used for optional user authentication and Firestore profile synchronization.
* **Google Play Services:** Used for app delivery and core Android platform services.

These third-party providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy and security standards.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

DermaMind AI is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 18 depending on regional age restrictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

---

## 7. Medical & Health Disclaimer

**DermaMind AI is designed strictly for general educational and wellness informational purposes.**

* It does **not** provide clinical diagnosis, medical treatment, or prescription advice.
* AI-generated observations cannot replace professional medical evaluation by a licensed physician, dermatologist, or healthcare provider.
* Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional with any medical questions or emergencies.

---

## 8. Advertisements & Financial Data

* **No Ads:** DermaMind AI does not display third-party advertisements or integrate advertising SDKs.
* **No Financial Services:** DermaMind AI does not provide banking, loans, investments, cryptocurrency, or gambling services.

---

If you have questions, feedback, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to:

* **Email:** `support@dermamind.ai`
